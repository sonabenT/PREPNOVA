# PREPNOVA 🚀

## AI-Driven Job Preparation Platform

PrepNova is an AI-powered job preparation platform designed to help students and job seekers prepare for their careers through personalized interview practice, resume analysis, and career guidance.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Provide role-based mock interviews
- Analyze resumes and identify improvement areas
- Generate personalized career roadmaps
- Help users improve their interview readiness
- Provide an organized platform for job preparation

## ✨ Features

### 🤖 AI-Powered Mock Interviews
Practice interviews based on the selected job role and receive useful feedback.

### 📄 Resume Analysis
Upload a resume and get insights into skills, strengths, and areas that can be improved.

### 🗺️ Personalized Career Roadmap
Get a structured roadmap based on your career goal, skills, and target role.

### 👤 Role-Based Preparation
Preparation content and interview questions can be customized according to the selected job role.
